# Contributing

This is an open, living framework. Thoughtful disagreement is as welcome as agreement — if a pillar is wrong, incomplete, or missing, make the case.

## Ways to contribute

- **Case studies.** Real (or realistic, anonymized) agent systems mapped onto the ten pillars. These are the most valuable contributions.
- **Counter-examples.** Production agents that succeed *without* a pillar, or fail *despite* having one. Evidence that challenges the framework strengthens it.
- **Citations.** Additional peer-reviewed or primary-source references. Every empirical claim should trace to a verifiable source — no unverified or AI-hallucinated citations.
- **Corrections.** Factual errors, broken links, outdated claims.
- **Translations.** Of the white paper or the rubric.

## Ground rules

1. **Cite primary sources.** Link to the paper, standard, or official documentation — not a blog summary of it. Verify arXiv IDs and dates before submitting.
2. **Keep the positioning honest.** This is a *synthesis*. Do not add language claiming any underlying discipline is newly invented here.
3. **Prose is the author's voice.** Keep contributions clear, plain, and free of marketing tone.
4. **One change per pull request** where practical, so discussion stays focused.

## How to propose a change

1. Open an **issue** describing the change and why it improves the framework.
2. For substantive edits, reference the section and cite your sources.
3. Submit a **pull request** linked to the issue.

## What gets rejected

- Unverifiable statistics or citations.
- Vendor promotion dressed as guidance.
- Scope creep beyond production agent engineering (e.g., unrelated robotics/AV depth) unless it earns its place with a clear pillar mapping.

Thank you for helping make this more useful and more correct.
